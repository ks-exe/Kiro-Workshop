# Product Overview

AI Study Buddy is a web-based chatbot that helps students learn any topic through guided conversation. It uses the Socratic method — asking guiding questions rather than giving direct answers.

## Core Features

- **Chat**: Students ask questions and receive guided, encouraging responses from an AI tutor
- **Quiz Generation**: AI generates multiple-choice quizzes based on the conversation context
- **Flashcards**: AI creates study flashcards summarizing key concepts discussed

## Key Behaviors

- The AI never gives direct answers to homework/exam questions
- Responses are limited to 5 sentences and use simple vocabulary
- Every explanatory response includes a quiz question
- Incorrect answers receive hints, not corrections
- Every response ends with an encouraging phrase
